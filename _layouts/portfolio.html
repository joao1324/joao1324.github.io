Bio

I am a robotics engineer with an MEng from Heriot-Watt University, where I specialised in autonomous systems, perception, and embedded control. During my degree I contributed to an autonomous pleural thoracentesis research project - a medical robotics system published at ICARM 2025 - and completed a final-year dissertation on imitation learning for real-time robotic tracking. My project experience spans medical robotics, sustainable underwater systems, and intelligent navigation, giving me a broad foundation in both hardware and software integration. I am currently enrolled in the RAINZ CDT pursuing a PhD in Robotics and AI, focused on robot perception, manipulation, and decision-making in complex environments.

Objectives & Research Interests

Passionate about building systems that can perceive and act reliably in the real world, my interests span medical robotics, human-robot interaction, and AI-driven perception and control. I am actively looking for research collaborations and engineering projects where I can apply and grow these skills.
